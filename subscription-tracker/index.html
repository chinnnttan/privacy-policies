Privacy Policy

Subscription Tracker

Last Updated: November 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Subscription Tracker ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what data is handled, by whom, and for what purpose when you use our Android mobile application. It is written to be transparent and compliant with Google Play Store requirements, AdMob policies, GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and LGPD.

2. App Permissions

The app requests only the following permissions — all are necessary for core features:

  • INTERNET: Required to load advertisements, fetch remote app configuration, receive push notifications, and power optional AI features.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to deliver subscription renewal reminders and push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • AD_ID (Advertising ID): Required by Google AdMob to serve relevant advertisements. You can reset or opt out of ad personalization at any time in your Android device settings.

The app does not request or use permissions for camera, microphone, GPS location, contacts, or call logs.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Your subscription data and app preferences are stored locally on your device using Android's local database (Room) and DataStore. This data never leaves your device:

  • Subscription details you enter (service names, amounts, billing cycles, renewal dates, categories, and notes)
  • Theme (light / dark / system default) and display currency preferences
  • Notification and renewal reminder preferences
  • App lock / biometric lock preference (biometric verification is handled entirely by the Android system; the app never accesses or stores your biometric data)
  • Your ad consent choices (stored locally via Google User Messaging Platform)

4. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party SDKs

We do not build our own analytics or tracking infrastructure. However, the following third-party SDKs integrated in the app may collect limited, pseudonymous technical and advertising data automatically:

  • Android Advertising ID (GAID): A resettable, device-level identifier used by Google AdMob to serve relevant ads. You can reset or opt out of personalization at any time (see Section 11).
  • App usage events: Screen views and interaction events collected by Firebase Analytics to help understand how features are used in aggregate.
  • FCM registration token: A device-level push token generated by Firebase Cloud Messaging, used solely to deliver notifications to your device. It is not shared with advertisers.
  • Remote configuration: Firebase Remote Config fetches non-personal app configuration values.
  • Ad interaction data: Impressions, clicks, and viewability signals collected by Google AdMob to measure ad performance.

Firebase Analytics is disabled in debug and beta builds and only active in production (release) builds.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name, surname, or physical address
  • Your email address or phone number
  • Bank account numbers, credit card details, or payment credentials
  • Precise or approximate GPS location
  • Device contacts, photos, or local files
  • Microphone or camera input
  • Your biometric data (fingerprint or face data)

6. How Data Is Used

Data handled by the app and integrated SDKs is used for the following specific purposes only:

  • Providing the service: Storing and managing your subscriptions locally and scheduling renewal reminders.
  • Usage analytics: Firebase Analytics provides aggregate, anonymized statistics on which features are used, helping us improve the app experience.
  • Push notifications: FCM tokens are used to deliver notifications directly to your device.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob uses the Android Advertising ID to display ads. With your consent, ads may be personalized. Without consent, only non-personalized ads are shown.
  • Consent management: Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) records and enforces your privacy choices for ad personalization.

7. Advertising & Ad Personalization

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may show:

  • Personalized ads — based on your Android Advertising ID and interests — only if you have provided explicit consent via the in-app consent form (required for EEA/UK users).
  • Non-personalized ads — contextual ads not based on your profile — when consent is not given or has been revoked.

8. International Data Transfers

Pseudonymous technical and advertising identifiers processed by Google LLC may be transferred to and stored in the United States or other countries where data protection laws may differ from your country of residence. Google safeguards these transfers using approved mechanisms including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

9. Data Retention

  • Locally stored subscriptions and preferences are retained on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app.
  • Firebase Analytics event data is retained for a maximum of 14 months, as per Google's standard retention policy.
  • FCM registration tokens are managed by Google and refreshed automatically by the Android system.

10. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

11. Your Controls & Choices

  • Revoke ad consent: You can update or revoke your ad consent choices from the in-app privacy/consent settings at any time.
  • Opt out of personalized ads (Android device-wide): Device Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Reset Advertising ID: Device Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID — this gives you a new random identifier.
  • Disable notifications: Go to Device Settings → Apps → Subscription Tracker → Notifications and toggle them off.
  • Clear all app data: Go to Device Settings → Apps → Subscription Tracker → Storage → Clear Data. This removes all local subscriptions, preferences, and consent choices.
  • Uninstall the app: Removes all locally stored data immediately.

12. Security

All network communication in the app uses HTTPS where available. Locally stored subscriptions and preferences are protected by Android's standard app sandboxing — no other app can access them. However, no method of data transmission or storage is 100% secure.

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
